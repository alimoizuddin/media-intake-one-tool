STAR - SPOKEN, ABOUT 60 SECONDS
-------------------------------

SITUATION
I run a transcription pipeline for lecture recordings - around 30
files a month, roughly three hours each, so about 90 hours of
content. The front end was a paid online converter at 999 rupees a
year. A three-hour lecture took about an hour to convert, plus
another 15 to 20 minutes to upload and download. Transfers failed
partway often enough that I had to babysit them, and anything over
the size cap I had to split by hand before it would even upload.

TASK
The conversion was pure overhead. It produced nothing I couldn't
produce locally, and it was the slowest step in a pipeline where
everything downstream was already automated.

ACTION
I found that the audio in these files didn't need converting at all
- it needed extracting. So I built a drag-and-drop tool on FFmpeg
that pulls the audio stream directly, and in the same pass produces
a 16 kHz mono, loudness-normalised file tuned for transcription. I
added automatic splitting at 199 MB, because NotebookLM rejects
anything over 200 - so the manual splitting step disappeared too.
It took me an hour to build.

RESULT
Turnaround per lecture went from about 75 minutes to roughly 40
seconds - over a hundred times faster. At my volume that's around
38 hours of turnaround removed per month, close to 460 hours a
year, and the 999 rupee licence went away. It paid for its own
build time on the very first file. It also runs entirely offline,
so uploads can't fail and my content never leaves my machine.


PAR - TIGHT, WRITTEN
--------------------

PROBLEM
A paid cloud converter was the slowest step in my transcription
pipeline: ~75 minutes of turnaround per 3-hour lecture across ~30
lectures a month, with frequent failed uploads and manual
file-splitting to stay under a 200 MB ingest cap.

ACTION
Built a drag-and-drop FFmpeg tool that extracts audio locally
instead of converting it in the cloud, emits a transcription-ready
16 kHz mono track in the same pass, and auto-splits output at
199 MB to fit the downstream cap. One hour to build.

RESULT
Turnaround per lecture fell from ~75 minutes to ~40 seconds
(>100x), removing ~460 hours of annual turnaround and a 999
rupee/year licence. Payback on the first file. Fully offline - no
failed transfers, no third-party handling of source material.
